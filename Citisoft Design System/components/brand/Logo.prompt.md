Brand logo lockup. Pick `variant` by background; point `basePath` at wherever the logo PNGs are served from relative to your page.

```jsx
<Logo height={36} basePath="assets" />          {/* full color on light */}
<Logo variant="white" basePath="../assets" />   {/* on a dark hero */}
```

- Files: `citisoft-logo.png`, `citisoft-logo-white.png`, `citisoft-logo-slate.png`. The sparkle + chevron motif is the brand's signature glyph.
