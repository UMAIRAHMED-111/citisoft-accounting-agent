User/account avatar; shows the image, or initials on the brand gradient when no `src`.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Dana Ruiz" src={url} />
<Avatar name="Marcus Lee" size="sm" />
<Avatar name="Acme Steel" square size="lg" />
```

- **size**: `xs` 24 / `sm` 32 / `md` 40 / `lg` 52. **square** for org/brand logos.
